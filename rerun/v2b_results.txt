Q1. Energy exchange (background only). Inferred rho_DE,eff = rho_DE + rho_c - rho_c0 a^-3
================================================================================================
Analytic: 1 + w_eff = [(1+w) rho_DE + D] / (rho_DE + D), D = rho_c - rho_c0 a^-3. An apparent phantom past needs D < 0,
i.e. dark matter LESS dense in the past than a^-3 extrapolation: energy must flow DE -> DM (Q < 0 in rho_c' + 3H rho_c = -Q).
